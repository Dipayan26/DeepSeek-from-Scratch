🎯 Here is the exact LoRA configuration you will use after training

Once the base model is trained, you will do:

from peft import LoraConfig, get_peft_model

lora_config = LoraConfig(
    r=16,
    lora_alpha=32,
    lora_dropout=0.05,
    bias="none",
    target_modules=[
        "q_proj", "k_proj", "v_proj", "o_proj",
        "up_proj", "down_proj", "gate_proj"
    ]
)

lora_model = get_peft_model(model, lora_config)


This will automatically add LoRA adapters to:

attention projections

MoE projections

MLP projections

and optionally the LM head
